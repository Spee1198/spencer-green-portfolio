How I work

Fail safe

On any error, outputs turn off and the bench goes idle, with cleanup in every path.

Every wait has a timeout

A test never hangs on hardware that stops answering.

Test the real path

I trigger a fault with the input that causes it, never by lowering the trip threshold.

Operator text names who or what

A failure screen says what failed, in plain words that name the unit or part.
